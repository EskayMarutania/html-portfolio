About Me

Born in Cincinnati, Ohio a curious and inquisitive individual I have always been interested in the mechanisms that make our life possible, the history and culture that defines me and the connections that create the society we live in. My journey up until this point has been anything but traditional and I consider my capacity to learn and adapt one of my greatest strengths. In addition to adaptability and pattern recognition I pride myself on the respect for others that I was raised to value. Combining these qualities has allowed me to navigate professional communities with confidence while delivering high-quality work.

Having worked jobs in various industries ranging from hospitality to technology I have gained valuable experience with a diverse skill set. Analytical thinking, creative problem-solving and effective communication are combined with development skills, technical expertise and strategic project management to ultimately deliver solutions with actual impact

In addition to a passion for all things science and technology, I also enjoy playing video games, reading and writing science fiction and fantasy stories, and traveling to countries outside of the United States. One of my goals for my 30s is to touch every ocean except maybe the arctic and antarctic; only the Indian and Southern oceans remain. Outside of work I am also pursuing a degree in mathematics with a focus on applied mathematics and modeling with the hopes to eventually be a patent attorney or agent. I aim to combine my love of science and technology with my passion for writing and communication to help inventors of historically marginalized communities protect their intellectual property, innovations and ideas.
